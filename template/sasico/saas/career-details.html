Job Description:

We’re looking for a creative and detail-oriented Product Designer to join our team. You’ll be responsible for designing intuitive, user-centered digital experiences across web and mobile platforms. From wireframes to final UI, you’ll collaborate with cross-functional teams to turn ideas into elegant and effective design solutions.

From concept to execution, you’ll be involved in every stage of the process—bringing creativity, strategic thinking, and technical expertise to the table. If you thrive in fast-paced, collaborative environment and have a strong eye for design and usability

Key Responsibilities:

  • Translate product requirements and user needs into wireframes, mockups, and interactive prototypes
  • Design clean, functional, and visually appealing UI for web apps, dashboards, or SaaS platforms
  • Collaborate closely with developers, product managers, and stakeholders throughout the design process
  • Conduct user research, usability testing, and feedback analysis to inform design decisions
  • Maintain consistency by applying design systems, style guides, and UI standards
  • Deliver development-ready Figma files with clearly organized layers and annotations

Requirements:

  • 2–5+ years of experience in UI/UX or Product Design
  • Proficiency in Figma (or similar tools like Sketch, Adobe XD)
  • Strong portfolio showcasing web app, SaaS, or dashboard projects
  • Solid understanding of UX principles, responsive design, and user behavior
  • Good communication skills and ability to take feedback constructively
  • Bonus: knowledge of front-end development (HTML/CSS/JS)

What We Offer:

  • Collaborative and creative work environment
  • Opportunity to design products that make real user impact
  • Competitive compensation and room for growth
  • Fast-moving, creative work culture
